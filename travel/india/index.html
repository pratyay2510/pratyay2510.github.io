INDIA
South Asia  ·  Pratyay Dutta

A country that
rewrites itself

Every state its own world. Every road a different language.
I grew up here and I am still only beginning to understand it.

India is not a single destination. It is a subcontinent that contains multitudes — the cold-desert silence of Ladakh and the humid chaos of Kolkata, the pink sandstone of Jaipur and the green valleys of Himachal. I have been lucky enough to see some of it. What follows are notes from the places that stayed with me.
